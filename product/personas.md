# Personas

These are proto-personas: built from the original team's SRS and use case documents and from typical university workflows, not from interviews. Each one should be validated with 3 to 5 real users before it drives major decisions.

---

## 1. Priya, undergraduate student (semester 5, B.Tech CSE)

**Goals**
- Graduate on time with a CGPA good enough for placements.
- Pick electives that fit her credit budget and interests.

**How she manages records today**
- Checks results on the portal once per semester.
- Keeps her own spreadsheet to work out how many credits she still needs.

**Pain points**
- Cannot see her CGPA trend at a glance; only one semester at a time.
- Has to calculate credits remaining to graduate by hand, and is never sure she has it right.
- Hears about a failing grade only when results are published.

**What success looks like**
- Opens one page and knows: current CGPA, direction of travel, credits left.

---

## 2. Dr. Mehta, faculty (teaches 3 courses per semester)

**Goals**
- Enter marks quickly and correctly.
- Help students who are struggling before the final exam.

**How he manages records today**
- Enters component marks per course in SARMS.
- Keeps an informal mental list of "students to watch".

**Pain points**
- No class-level summary: average, spread, who is below the pass line.
- No easy way to compare this semester's cohort with the last one.
- Finding struggling students means scanning a long marks table.

**What success looks like**
- Sees a short, ranked list of students who need attention in each course, mid-semester.

---

## 3. Mr. Rao, academic admin staff (examinations office)

**Goals**
- Publish results on time with zero errors.
- Report department performance to the dean.

**How he manages records today**
- Uses SARMS to publish results and promote students.
- Exports data and builds charts in Excel for the dean's review.

**Pain points**
- Department reports are built by hand every semester.
- Cannot quickly spot a course with an unusual fail rate.
- No evidence of which SARMS features staff and students actually use.

**What success looks like**
- A dashboard he can screenshot for the dean, instead of a spreadsheet he rebuilds each term.
